Default Payment Method on Customers & Sales Orders
for Odoo

The Kaizen – Default Payment Method on Customers & Sales Orders module streamlines your sales process by automatically applying each customer’s preferred payment method to their sales orders. With this add-on, you can set a default Cash or Bank journal directly on the customer record. Every time a new Sales Order is created, the payment method is prefilled from the customer’s default — reducing manual data entry, preventing mistakes, and ensuring consistent accounting practices. This module is ideal for companies handling high volumes of sales or working with repeat customers, where efficiency and accuracy in payment handling are critical.

Default Payment Method on Customers & Sales Orders

Gain complete control and accuracy over customer payment handling—automatically capture and apply each customer’s default Cash or Bank journal without adding extra steps to your sales workflow. At order creation, sales teams are guided by pre-set, customer-specific payment methods, eliminating guesswork and manual selection errors. Built-in consistency ensures every Sales Order flows into accounting with the correct journal, while managers benefit from standardized, cleaner financial records. Whether managing hundreds of repeat customers or operating across multiple branches, you get faster order entry, reduced reconciliation errors, and reliable data that keeps both sales and finance aligned. Perfect for high-volume trading, distribution, and services businesses where speed and accuracy are critical.

  • Set Default Sale Payment Method on Customer
  • Automatic Payment Method on Sales Order

Set Default Sale Payment Method on Customer

Define each customer’s preferred payment method once and let Odoo handle the rest. From the Sales & Purchase tab of the customer form, select either Cash or Bank as the default Sale Payment Method. Every new Sales Order created for this customer will automatically inherit this choice, ensuring faster order entry, fewer errors, and consistent accounting across your sales process.

Automatic Payment Method on Sales Order

When you create a new Sales Order, the Payment Method field is automatically filled with the customer’s default Cash or Bank journal. This removes the need for manual selection, accelerates order creation, and ensures accounting consistency from the very first step. Users can still adjust the method if required, but by default, every order inherits the correct journal—keeping sales and finance perfectly aligned.
