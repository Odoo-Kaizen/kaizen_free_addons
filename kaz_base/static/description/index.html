Base Utilities (Arabic Amount-in-Words, Record Insights & Dev Tools)
for Odoo

Base Utilities (Arabic Amount‑in‑Words, Record Insights & Dev Tools) by Kaizen Principles helps finance and reporting teams produce compliant Arabic documents and keep day‑to‑day accounting simple. It adds native Arabic amount‑in‑words and Arabic‑Indic numerals to QWeb reports, a one‑click Record Inspector that surfaces who changed what and when (from audit logs and chatter), and safer sequences that fail fast with clear guidance. For implementers, it bundles practical developer tools—server‑action helpers, external‑ID utilities, hierarchical sort, caching, and a lightweight /ping healthcheck—for faster, cleaner customizations. The result is fewer manual edits, quicker investigations, and smoother deployments across invoices, bills, journal entries, and custom reports—without introducing new menus or complex workflows.

What's New in Base Utilities (Arabic Amount-in-Words, Record Insights & Dev Tools) for Odoo

Converts amounts to Arabic words (supports SAR, AED, JOD, BHD, SYP)

Warns clearly when a required sequence is missing

Adds ready‑made helpers for reports and automations (Arabic words & digits)
